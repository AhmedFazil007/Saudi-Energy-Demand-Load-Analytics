E-Commerce & Last-Mile Delivery

An end-to-end look at sales, basket value and delivery reliability in a fictional Saudi e-commerce business.

400KOrders analyzed
+31.8%2025 order growth
+2.1%2025 delivered-sales growth

The business question

When orders increase, is the business actually performing better?

How I approached it

  • Analyzed 400,000 simulated orders spanning 2022–2025 alongside customer and delivery records.
  • Compared delivered sales with order counts and average delivered order value.
  • Reviewed delivery timeliness, product categories, sales channels and city-level concentration.

Key findings

  • Delivered average order value fell by approximately 20.1% between 2024 and 2025.
  • About 29.9% of completed deliveries were late in the modeled data.
  • Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam together represented around 59.1% of delivered sales over the four-year period.

Why it matters

Order growth alone can overstate progress when average basket value weakens. Delivery reliability is another important part of the customer experience, but the simulated carrier-level late rates are similar, so a single carrier cannot reasonably be singled out.

Recommendations I'd explore

  • Investigate basket size, items per order, discounting and product mix before increasing acquisition spend.
  • Segment late deliveries by city, warehouse and promised delivery window.
  • Test bundles or cross-selling on a limited group and compare contribution after fulfillment costs.

How I'd measure potential impact

  • Delivered average order value
  • On-time delivery rate
  • Contribution after delivery costs

These are suggested measures for future testing, not results from implemented changes.

Scope and limitations

The synthetic data generator changes items per order over time, contributing to the observed decline in average order value. No actual business improvements were implemented.

This independent project uses synthetic data. The figures are not official Saudi statistics, and no financial or operational improvement is claimed as achieved.
