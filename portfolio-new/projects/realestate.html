Real Estate Market Intelligence

A property-market case study examining transaction volume, property mix, pricing and financing.

The business question

Does rising transaction value mean more activity or simply larger deals?

How I approached it

  • Analyzed 12,000 synthetic transactions across ten cities from 2021–2025.
  • Separated transaction count, total value and median price.
  • Compared property types and financing methods across the simulated market.

Key findings

  • Annual transaction value increased 16.4% between 2021 and 2025, while both years recorded 2,337 deals.
  • Villas represented 24.6% of transactions but 37.4% of transaction value.
  • Riyadh accounted for approximately SAR 1.72B, or 13.8% of modeled total value.

Why it matters

Higher transaction value does not automatically indicate a busier market. Property type, deal size and pricing can change the total independently of volume.

Recommendations I'd explore

  • Compare city-level transaction count with median price and property mix.
  • Separate high-value segments from high-volume segments.
  • Validate any location shortlist with real closed deals, leads and conversion rates.

How I'd measure potential impact

  • Transaction volume
  • Median transaction price
  • Price per square metre and lead-to-close rate

These are suggested measures for future testing, not results from implemented changes.

Scope and limitations

These synthetic transactions are not official Saudi property-market statistics and cannot establish actual price appreciation or investment returns.

This independent project uses synthetic data. The figures are not official Saudi statistics, and no financial or operational improvement is claimed as achieved.
